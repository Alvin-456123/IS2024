# Understanding Data Centers and Cloud Computing Introduction

## A. What is Data Centers?

A data center is a physical facility that organizations use to house their critical applications, servers, storage systems, and networking equipment. It's designed to store, manage, and process large volumes of data. Data centers provide the infrastructure necessary for businesses to operate and deliver services such as cloud computing, hosting websites, and supporting applications.
